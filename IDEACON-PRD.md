IDEACON — Product Requirements Document

_Idea + Connection — the platform that links novel ideas to the industries built to use them._

# 1\. Vision & Problem Statement

Innovative ideas are created constantly — by independent inventors, researchers, and everyday people — but most never reach the companies that could turn them into real products, services, or market advantages. Meanwhile, companies, from early-stage startups to established enterprises, are often searching for fresh angles, unique selling points, and paths to scale, but have no reliable channel to discover ideas outside their own walls.

IDEACON closes this gap: a global platform that captures a limitless pool of novel ideas, evaluates and organizes them, and connects them to the right niche or industry, giving both creators and companies a trusted place to find each other.

# 2\. Target Users

## Idea Creators

- Independent inventors and innovators
- General public (crowdsourced submissions)
- Vetted experts and researchers

## Companies

- Startups and small businesses
- Large enterprises and corporates
- Both segments targeted simultaneously from launch, globally from day one

# 3\. Core Value Propositions

## For companies

- Access ready-made ideas to license or adopt
- Discover market gaps and emerging trends
- Connect directly with the creators behind promising ideas

## For creators

- Visibility for ideas that would otherwise go unseen
- A protected path to monetize or partner around their idea
- Access to the right industry, not just an audience — the right one

# 4\. Key Features & Flows

## 4.1 Idea Submission

Open to all creator types (public, independent inventors, vetted experts). Submissions are not fully disclosed publicly — ideas are teased via niche, uniqueness, and scaling potential rather than full detail.

## 4.2 Idea Evaluation & Quality Control

- Expert review board evaluates submissions
- AI scoring assesses originality, feasibility, and market fit
- Community voting/rating — planned as a phase 2 addition once the platform has scale and trust established

## 4.3 Categorization & Discovery

Ideas are filterable by:

- Industry sector (tech, health, fashion, etc.)
- Problem type (efficiency, sustainability, cost reduction, etc.)
- Stage of idea (concept, prototype, ready-to-scale)

## 4.4 Matching

A hybrid approach combining:

- AI-powered auto-suggestions linking ideas to relevant niches/industries
- Manual browsing and search by companies
- Curation and oversight by the IDEACON team

## 4.5 Onboarding

- Separate onboarding flows for idea creators vs. companies
- Companies may be invited or verified separately for trust/quality assurance
- IDEACON team retains discretion to review or modify onboarding paths as needed

## 4.6 Connection & Deal Flow

All company-creator connections are brokered by the IDEACON team — no direct open messaging. This preserves control over quality, protects creators, and lets IDEACON manage the handoff professionally.

## 4.7 IP & Ownership Protection

Before any full idea detail is shared:

- NDA required to view full details
- Timestamped record kept as proof of origin for every submission
- Built-in legal agreement templates to formalize licensing/partnership terms

## 4.8 Creator Compensation Models

Flexible by idea, decided case-by-case:

- Paid licensing/royalty arrangements
- Direct partnership or equity (rare, for select high-value ideas)
- Terms otherwise customized to the creator's preference — refine, modify, or approve as the deal develops

# 5\. Business Model

Flexible and multi-pronged, with the IDEACON team retaining judgment per T&Cs:

- Subscription plans for companies (tiered access to idea pools, filters, matching)
- Commission on successful matches/deals
- Freemium model — free basic access, paid premium features
- Team discretion to apply the best-fit model per account or deal

# 6\. Trust & Credibility Strategy

Especially critical in early stages to attract both creators and companies:

- Publish success stories and case studies of completed matches
- Grow and showcase idea count and diversity across industries
- Build visible company partnerships/logos as social proof
- Lean on the brokered, NDA-protected model itself as a trust signal — IDEACON positions itself as a curated agency, not an open marketplace

# 7\. Go-to-Market Approach

- Launch targeting both startups and large enterprises at once, rather than sequencing one before the other
- Global scope from day one — no initial regional restriction
- Early growth likely driven by case studies and visible successful matches, since trust is the primary adoption barrier for both sides (creators fear idea theft; companies fear low-quality submissions)

# 8\. Open Questions / Areas to Refine Further

- Exact criteria/rubric for expert review board scoring
- Specific tiers and pricing for subscription/freemium model
- Volume/throughput plan — how many submissions the review board + AI pipeline can realistically process at scale
- Legal jurisdiction handling for a global platform (varying IP law, contract enforceability)
- Whether/when to introduce community voting, and how to prevent gaming of ratings once introduced

# 9\. Recommendations

- **Start with the brokered model as a moat, not a bottleneck.** It's labor-intensive early on, but it's what makes creators trust you with unpublished ideas and what makes companies trust the quality bar. Automate matching suggestions, but keep humans in the final handoff loop until the brand is established.
- **Prioritize the case-study engine early.** Since trust is your biggest early barrier on both sides, your first 10–20 successful matches matter more than your total idea count. Consider incentivizing a small group of high-quality creators and a handful of design-partner companies pre-launch to generate your first proof points.
- **Keep the compensation model flexible but templated.** Fully case-by-case deals will be hard to scale operationally. Build 2–3 standard deal templates (e.g. "licensing," "revenue share," "advisory partnership") that cover most cases, with true custom deals as the exception, not the default.
- **Delay community voting deliberately.** Introducing public ratings too early — before you have enough idea volume and quality control — risks a race-to-the-bottom on visible ideas. Phase 2 is the right call.
- **Global-from-day-one needs a legal backbone before scale.** Given the IP-protection promise (NDA, proof of origin, licensing templates) is core to your pitch, get this legally solid in at least 2–3 major jurisdictions before actively marketing in them — a broken promise here would be fatal to trust.
